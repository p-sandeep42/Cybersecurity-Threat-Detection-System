Three steps to your first event

  1. Set THREATWATCH_INGESTION_KEY in your demo backend's environment.
  2. Run the demo on port 5001 and attempt a login. Its background sender reports the outcome.
  3. Open Overview to verify the last received time, then Events to inspect the record.

HTTP endpoint: POST http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/v1/events

Headers: Authorization: Bearer <key> and Content-Type: application/json.

Use the complete event contract in docs/plan/02-architecture.md. The backend must derive outcomes and source IPs. Do not send passwords or tokens.
